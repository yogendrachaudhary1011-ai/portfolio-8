ACTIVITY TRACKING ECOSYSTEM

Perro’s most powerful module is the activity tracker. Beautifully visual, flexible, and data rich.

Multiple ways to track

  • Perro wearable device integration

  • Google Fit / Apple Health sync

  • Manual logging

Persistent Bottom Sheet

Available anywhere in the app. Swipe up to start, pause, or resume activities, launch mindfulness sessions, or track ongoing progress.

Four swipeable categories

  • Overview: Holistic daily/weekly activity

  • Time: Active minutes, trends

  • Distance: Steps, routes, walks

  • Playtime: Joyous moments captured

The interface uses progress rings, gradients, cards, and smooth transitions to make data instantly readable and encouraging.

Perro&You

Perro isn’t just about physical activity. It’s also about emotional connection.

Mindfulness & Training Content

  • Guided videos

  • Interactive audio sessions

  • Step-by-step practice activities

  • Bite-sized training tips

Curated 'For You' Feed (Premium)

AI-based recommendations tailored to your dog’s behaviour, age, progress, and needs. This section adds heart and meaning to the overall experience.

SOCIAL & COMMUNITY

Perro helps users connect with their local dog community:

Explore dog-friendly locations on a map

View user reviews, ratings, and photos

Share experiences directly in the app

Help dog parents discover trusted places

This turns Perro into a connective, community-driven app, not just a personal tracker.

REWARDS & PERRO HEALTH

Perro helps users connect with their local dog community:

XP System

Every interaction earns XP. Adding Routines, Activities, Mindfulness sessions, Social posting, everything is rewarding.

Badges & Levels

Milestones unlock badges and level-ups, creating satisfying long-term progression.

Woof Rewards

Redeem real discounts from brands like ASDA, Pooch & Mutt, Perfect Pet Insurance, and more.

Perro Health (Premium Plan)

  • Exclusive partner discounts

  • Curated “For You” mindfulness feed

  • Advanced analytics

  • Vet chat & on-demand guidance and many more benefits
