Design Process

Every project starts with questions, not screens. I research, map the problem space, then design systems that scale - always looping back with teams and users until the experience feels inevitable.

Systems Thinking

Reusable components, design consistency, 81+ component variants, and tokenized UI patterns that keep interfaces coherent.

User Research

User flows, workflow mapping, and usability testing to ground every design decision in real feedback, usability reviews, and clarity.

Interaction Design

Interface design, typography, layout hierarchy, and UI components prioritizing clarity, usability, and consistent interaction patterns.

Cross-functional Collab

Proficient in Figma, Adobe Photoshop, and Illustrator, delivering comprehensive developer handoff specs and collaborating closely with engineers.

Experience

I've worked with companies and clients, both in agency settings and as a freelancer. I enjoy collaborating with clients who appreciate the importance of good design.
